What we heard

Your Current Challenges

The points raised during the session, each paired with how BigChange addresses it.

Challenge

Outgrown the current system

The business has scaled quickly since January, with 10 engineers today, 12 by early November and a plan for 14 to 15 by December. Gas Engineer Software is no longer sophisticated enough for where the business is heading.

BigChange solution

One platform that scales with you

Scheduling, the engineer app, tracking, timesheets, stock, CRM, alerts and reporting all sit under one roof, so adding engineers and office staff is simply adding users, not adding systems.

Challenge

Job reports not syncing back

When engineers lose signal, job reports don't come through. The team runs evening and morning checks to make sure every report from the day before has arrived, and this becomes a bigger risk with more contracts, engineers and volume.

BigChange solution

Fully offline app with automatic re-sync

Engineers complete jobs, take photos, collect signatures and take payment with no signal. The app keeps looking for signal and syncs back to the office automatically, with no button to press and no reports to chase.

Challenge

Reporting relies on spreadsheets

Seeing jobs outstanding awaiting parts, upcoming jobs by postcode and engineer metrics currently means heavy spreadsheet work and manual importing.

BigChange solution

Built-in reports, filtered lists and JustAsk

A wide set of standard reports, filtered list views (awaiting parts, yet to be invoiced, by engineer) and JustAsk, which lets you ask questions of your data in plain English and build dashboards on first time fix, time on site and engineer performance.

Challenge

Manual van stock replenishment

Engineers carry van stock kits, but replenishment depends on engineers letting the office know what they've used on each job, which takes a lot of manual intervention to keep on point.

BigChange solution

Live van stock with reorder levels

Each van has a live stock record. Engineers scan a barcode to book parts to a job, stock is deducted automatically with the financials attached, and reorder levels alert the office before critical parts run out.

Challenge

Work orders arriving through every channel

Around 95% of the work is reactive insurance work. Work orders and enquiries arrive by email, WhatsApp and phone, all ending up in one inbox to be keyed in manually.

BigChange solution

Faster job creation, with Pulse on the way

Quick Job and the Unscheduled Jobs bucket make logging and allocating work fast today. Pulse, launching soon, is an AI tool that will create work orders from incoming emails and calls automatically and help schedule them.

Challenge

No access and aborted visits

Knocking on doors with no one in means wasted slots and lost earnings. Insurers ask for evidence such as call logs and a card through the door.

BigChange solution

Customer confirmations with a clear record

Branded confirmation emails let customers confirm, add information or request a reschedule. Unconfirmed jobs show red on the schedule, confirmed jobs turn green, and each confirmation is logged with date and time as evidence. Automated reminders go out the day before.

Challenge

No timesheets and separate tracking

Engineers don't complete timesheets today, and vehicle tracking runs in a separate system (Quartix) that doesn't talk to job data.

BigChange solution

Integrated tracking and automatic timesheets

BigChange installs its own trackers at no additional cost. Timesheets are captured through the app and reconciled against tracking data automatically, giving accurate time on site, start and finish times and driver behaviour scores.

Challenge

A small office team covering everything

Five office staff all chip in across scheduling, job progression, parts ordering, quoting and invoicing, relying on local knowledge to decide who goes where.

BigChange solution

Scheduling that does the thinking

Schedule Assistant suggests the best engineer by location, availability, skills and cost. Route optimisation reorders a day in one click, and automations move jobs awaiting parts into their own flagged folder so nothing is missed.

The demonstration

What We Showed You Today

Select an area on the left to see what was covered and how it helps.

The Engineer App

A simple, comprehensive app for Android or iOS, on personal phones or BigChange-supplied devices.

  • Everything in one app: engineers log on in the morning and off in the evening, with no paper or other apps needed in between.
  • Controlled visibility: you decide how far ahead engineers can see their work, from just the next job up to six weeks.
  • Job folders: every job carries site information, attachments, customer details and notes, ready before arrival.
  • Clear and intuitive: big, clear buttons designed to be picked up quickly by every engineer.
  • Card payments: take payment on site for direct-to-consumer, non-insurance work.

Live Job Folders & Offline Working

Everything done in the app is instantly visible in the office, and vice versa.

  • Instant updates: photos and videos recorded on site appear straight away in the job's attachments in the office.
  • Two-way changes: update a job description or date in the office and it reflects on the engineer's app immediately.
  • Fully offline: engineers complete jobs, take pictures, collect signatures and take payment with no signal.
  • Automatic re-sync: the app keeps looking for signal and syncs back automatically, with nothing for anyone to press.
  • No more report checks: no evening and morning sweeps to confirm every report has come through.

Unscheduled Jobs & Self-Serve

Let engineers pick up extra work themselves when a slot frees up.

  • Nearby work: after a no access or an early finish, engineers see outstanding jobs ordered by distance from their current location.
  • Self-allocate: they review the job, call the customer from the app and allocate it to themselves.
  • You stay in control: you decide which jobs sit in the bucket and which engineers can access it, with full visibility in the office.

In-App Messaging

Centralised communication between office and field.

  • Two-way messages: send messages to engineers and see replies in one place, so anyone in the office can pick a thread up.
  • Selectable responses: give engineers set options to reply with, for quick, consistent answers.
  • Automated notifications: alerts can message engineers automatically, for example when driving over the speed limit.

Subcontractor Jobs

Built for the subcontractors you already use.

  • Accept or refuse: subcontractors can accept or decline jobs allocated through the app.
  • Switch it off for employees: the accept and refuse buttons can be hidden entirely for your employed engineers.

Tracking, Timesheets & Absences

Vehicle tracking is part of the platform, not a separate system.

  • Trackers at no extra cost: BigChange installs its own hardware trackers in each vehicle.
  • Automatic timesheets: timesheets are captured in the app and reconciled against tracking data for accurate start, finish and on-site times.
  • Customer ETAs: send automatic alerts when an engineer is a set distance away, or a tracking link showing them on a map.
  • Driver behaviour scores: updated daily from numerous driving metrics and shown to each engineer.
  • Holiday requests: engineers request absences in the app; once approved, their calendar is blocked out automatically.

Enforced Workflows & Vehicle Checks

Your minimum standards, completed on every job.

  • Step-by-step process: for example, vehicle check, then risk assessment, then signature, then photos, before a job can close.
  • No skipping steps: engineers can't move to the next job, or even see it, until the current one is closed properly.
  • Real-time completion: work is completed as the day goes on, not caught up at home in the evening.
  • Vehicle checks: safety checks enforced daily, weekly or monthly, with defects raised automatically.

Van Stock Management

A live view of what is on every van.

  • Live van stock: engineers and the office see exactly what is on the back of each van.
  • Barcode scanning: scan a part to book it to a job, deducting stock and attaching the financials automatically.
  • Find the nearest part: search for a part and see which engineer nearby or which warehouse has it, and how far away.
  • Reorder levels: get alerted when critical parts drop below a set level.
  • Full movement history: see which parts were used on which job.

Site History, Expenses & Documents

Everything the engineer needs, without calling the office.

  • CRM access: engineers see basic site and contact details, with no financial information.
  • Previous visits: view past job reports and photos at a property before turning up for the first time.
  • Expenses: log expenses and receipts directly in the app.
  • Document library: categorised PDFs such as public liability insurance, van insurance and technical guides.

Fleet & Resources

One central home for your vehicle and engineer information.

  • Vehicle folders: MOT, road tax and insurance dates, plus attachments such as the V5 and service history.
  • Engineer folders: working hours, holiday allowance, hourly and overtime rates to cost jobs in real time.
  • Skills and certifications: record Gas Safe and other certificates with expiry dates, plus DBS checks and other attachments.
  • Daily snapshot: see hours worked and miles driven for each engineer on any given day.

Map View & Territories

See your engineers and your work in one place.

  • Find any vehicle or engineer: search by registration or by the engineer's logged-in device.
  • Journey detail: most recent journey with cost, mileage and speeding events, plus street view.
  • Jobs on the map: plot unscheduled work as flags to spot clusters, such as a group of jobs in one town, and plan accordingly.
  • Territories: draw out coverage areas, useful as you grow into new areas with subcontractors.

Alerts & Automations

A rules-based environment that takes repetitive tasks off the office team.

  • Awaiting parts: when an engineer flags parts are needed, a follow-up job is created, flagged red and filed in an awaiting parts folder automatically.
  • Quote follow-ups: automatically chase a quote that has been sent but not accepted after a set number of days.
  • Appointment reminders: text or email customers the day before a visit to cut no access visits.
  • Driver and location alerts: notify engineers about speeding, or the office when a vehicle reaches a set area such as a toll crossing.
  • Self-serve: thousands of possible automations, set up with you at the start and easy to build on over time. Emails are free; texts are charged per message.

The Schedule

Plan the work and see what is happening in the field as it happens.

  • Multiple views: daily, weekly, monthly and list views, with list filters such as jobs yet to be invoiced or jobs awaiting parts.
  • Live daily view: one lane per engineer, colour coded by status: complete, issue, in progress or booked.
  • Travel time: driving time shown between jobs using current traffic, with warnings when there isn't enough time.
  • Drag and drop: move jobs from the unscheduled bucket onto any engineer's diary in seconds.
  • Quick Job: log a job by postcode and job type, then schedule now or leave it unscheduled for later.

Schedule Assistant & Route Optimisation

The system works out who should go where.

  • Best engineer suggestions: scans diaries to show who is nearby and available, for example an engineer a mile away on Wednesday.
  • Skills matching: a boiler service only shows engineers with the right Gas Safe certification, and you can filter by specific skills.
  • Cost-aware: see the cost of sending each engineer based on their hourly rate.
  • Optimise route: reorder a day's jobs into the most efficient order in one click, while fixing set appointment times.
  • Fuel and time savings: a few pounds per day per engineer adds up to a substantial saving across the team.

Job Confirmations

Fewer wasted visits, and evidence when they do happen.

  • Branded confirmations: sent automatically or with one click, with your logo and optionally a picture of the engineer.
  • Customer self-service: customers confirm, update information, upload attachments or request a reschedule.
  • Red and green at a glance: unconfirmed jobs show red on the schedule and turn green once confirmed.
  • Logged evidence: the date and time of each confirmation is recorded, an extra backup for insurers on no access visits.

Reporting & JustAsk

Measure the business without spreadsheets.

  • Standard reports: a wide range of pre-built reports, including engineer time on site, first time fix and lateness.
  • Filtered lists: outstanding jobs awaiting parts, upcoming jobs and more, filtered by any parameter.
  • JustAsk: ask questions of your business data in plain English and build dashboards from the answers.
  • Measure change: see the real impact of any new process within weeks, rather than relying on feel.

Connected Contractors

Work seamlessly with partners who also use BigChange.

  • Direct job sharing: a partner such as Domestic & General can create a job and allocate it straight to AllGas North West.
  • Lands in your bucket: the job appears in your unscheduled jobs to accept and schedule to an engineer.
  • Updates flow back: once complete, the information goes straight back to the partner, with no emails needed.

UK Support & Setup

Real people in Leeds, for office and field.

  • Leeds-based team: everyone you work with at BigChange is based in our Leeds office.
  • Field support: engineers call BigChange support directly with app questions rather than the office.
  • Setup day in Leeds: members of the admin team can spend a full or half day with us to complete the bulk of the setup in one go.
